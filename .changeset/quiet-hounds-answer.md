A bot's delegate can now be answered when it asks for approval. A bot may hand work to another bot, and that bot may hand work on once more; the second hand-off used to raise its approval on a card you could never reach, so the work waited forever. That approval now appears on the card one level up, labelled with the bot that is asking, and answering it there unblocks the work.
