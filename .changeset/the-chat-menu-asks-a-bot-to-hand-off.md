The chat menu can ask a bot to hand off. A chat with one bot offers one row, "Hand off <bot>…", and a group offers a row per member under a "Hand off" label. The bot writes its handoff note and the chat records the note and where its reading of the conversation now starts; a bot that ends the turn without a note changes nothing, and the chat says the handoff did not happen.
