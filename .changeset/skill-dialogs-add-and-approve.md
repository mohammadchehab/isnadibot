Adding a skill and approving a Community one now have their own dialogs. **Add a skill** offers the three ways in — a provider's skill, a GitHub link, a `.zip` — in one dialog, and asks where it belongs: this space, or every space. A link that names no skill folder, or an archive that is too big or is not a zip, says so in the dialog before anything is sent. A Community skill then opens the approval dialog, which shows where the skill came from, what the bot will be told, and the files it brings, so nothing reaches a bot before you have read it.
