Bot avatars are now furry stitched critters: the same eight shapes and picker colours, with floppy ears, two feet, a sewn-on patch and big sparkly eyes. The face shows what the bot is doing: it grins while idle or working, looks puzzled with a question mark when it needs a decision from you, and looks sad with drooping ears and a tear when its session ended in an error. The mobile app draws the same avatars.
