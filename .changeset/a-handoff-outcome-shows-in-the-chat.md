A chat now shows what became of a hand off. The note a bot leaves is drawn as a card with the item, branch, what is done, the next step, who it is waiting on, and what not to do. The reset itself gets a row that says it happened, that it did not happen because the turn ended without a note, or that it was interrupted, so a reader never has to guess whether the context moved. A reset that landed offers Undo until that bot's next turn starts, and any handoff row can be hidden once it has been read.
