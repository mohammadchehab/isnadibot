Space settings reach the phone. Open a group chat's settings and select **Space**: the sheet names the space, summarizes it at a glance and lists the two settings a space has today, **Boss** and **Keep everyone busy**. **Boss** pushes the space's bots with the current boss checked; the switch sits under it.

The phone applies the same rules and uses the same words as the web page, so a space with no bots, a single bot or no boss says why a control cannot be used yet instead of offering one that does nothing. The rules behind those rows moved to the shared client runtime, which leaves the web page behaving exactly as before.
